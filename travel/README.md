# Wandermap

A travel tracker in the style of *been*. You mark the countries and US states you have visited, lived in or want to
visit, and see them on a 2D map and a 3D globe, with stats, a timeline and share images. It works fully offline,
needs no account and collects no data.

## Install (Android)

1. On your phone, open
   **https://github.com/kskerbergs/Instagram/releases/download/wandermap-latest/Wandermap.apk**
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to *install unknown apps*.
3. Open **Wandermap**.

Every build is signed with the same key, so a new APK installs over the old one and keeps your data.

## What's in it

| Area | Features |
|---|---|
| Map | 2D map (Robinson projection) and 3D globe; tap a country for details, long-press to toggle visited; pinch-zoom, pan, globe inertia, double-tap the globe to fly to a country; microstates drawn as tappable dots; filters by status, continent and year range |
| Places | Countries grouped by continent with "12 / 44" counts; US states (50 + DC, territories optional); search, filter, sort |
| Tap a country | The map zooms to it, outlines and tints it, and dims the rest. A big **I've been here** button adds it (with a check animation and haptic). Then you can switch to *Lived here*, add dates, or remove it. Closing the sheet glides the map back |
| Place sheet | One sheet everywhere: Visited / Lived / Wishlist / Clear, dated visits (month or exact day, optional end date, note), link to the US states map |
| Stats | World % by count and by land area, continent bars, US states, first and latest trip, most-visited country, timeline by year with a ▶ chronological map animation, achievements |
| Share | 1080×1350 or 1080×1920 PNG (map, map + stats, continents, year in travel; light or dark) to the Android share sheet or saved to Pictures |
| Compare | Share code / QR / `wandermap://c/…` link with your visited set (no server). Shows only you, only your friend, both, and wishlist countries in common |
| Profile | Multiple local profiles with their own colours, counting rules (UN 195 vs 249 with territories; 6 or 7 continents; US territories), colour by year, theme, JSON/CSV export and JSON import |

Counting rules are always shown, and changing one tells you the old and new denominator.

## Look and feel

The UI follows Apple's fluid-interface guidelines, adapted for the web (`js/motion.js`):

- Interruptible springs (damping ratio and response) instead of fixed-length animations. Everything you can touch can be grabbed mid-motion.
- The Place sheet tracks your finger 1:1, projects where a flick is heading, settles on a detent with your release speed, and rubber-bands at the top.
- Full-screen pages slide in from the right and leave the same way. Swipe from the left edge to go back.
- The flat map rubber-bands at its edges and glides on with momentum. The globe spins on and slows down like a scroll view.
- Feedback starts on touch-down: the country under your finger highlights, buttons press in, and changes give a light haptic tick.
- Countries blend to their new colour, the counter counts up, checks pop, and Stats cards cascade in.
- Translucent tab bar and map controls, iOS system colours, inset grouped lists, and size-specific letter spacing. The app follows the system font size.
- Undo replaces "are you sure?" for clearing a place or deleting a visit.
- Respects Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast.

## Not built yet

These parts of the spec are left out: cities and airports, video export, home-screen widget, in-app purchase (every
feature is unlocked), regions for countries other than the US, iOS build, and place-name localisation (search
already matches German and Russian names).

## Layout

- `web/`: the app (HTML/CSS/JS, no build step). `js/core.js` holds counting rules, stats and share codes; `js/map.js`
  draws the map and globe; `js/app.js` is the UI.
- `web/data/`, `web/vendor/`: generated by `tools/build-data.mjs` from Natural Earth (via world-atlas), us-atlas,
  world-countries, d3-geo and topojson-client. Re-run with `cd tools && npm install && npm run build`.
- `android/`: a WebView shell that serves `web/` from the APK and adds sharing, saving, the back button and links.
- `tests/core.test.mjs`: unit tests for every counting-rule combination and the share-code format
  (`node --test tests/core.test.mjs`).

To try it in a browser: `cd web && python3 -m http.server`, then open http://localhost:8000.

## Data sources

Natural Earth (public domain), US Census Bureau boundaries via us-atlas, country metadata from mledoze/countries
(ODbL), d3-geo and topojson-client (ISC), qrcode-generator (MIT).
